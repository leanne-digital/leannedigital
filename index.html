HOW IT WORKS

A clear process from first call to launch.

No mystery, no disappearing acts. You'll always know what we're working on and what comes next.

01
STEP ONE

Discovery Call

We'll hop on a quick call to get to know each other. You tell me about your business, your goals, and what you're looking for. I'll walk you through how I can help.

02
STEP TWO

Proposal

After our chat, I'll send over a custom proposal that lays everything out clearly. You'll see the scope of work, timeline, deliverables, and pricing upfront.

03
STEP THREE

Design

This is the fun part. I'll create custom designs based on our conversation and share them with you for feedback. We'll make sure everything feels right before moving ahead.

04
STEP FOUR

Development

Once the design is approved, my developer brings it to life with clean code, fast load times, and a smooth mobile experience.

05
STEP FIVE

Review & Testing

You'll get a full walk-through of the site before launch. We'll test everything, make final tweaks, and make sure it all feels good to go.

06
STEP SIX

Launch

We hit publish. You get a website or brand you're proud of, and I'm still here if you need updates, support, or want to grow even more down the road.

07
STEP SEVEN

Post-Launch Support

You'll have 30 days of support after launch for any questions, fixes, or small tweaks. After that, we offer maintenance and security plans to keep your site running smoothly, plus the option to book extra hours whenever you need them.
