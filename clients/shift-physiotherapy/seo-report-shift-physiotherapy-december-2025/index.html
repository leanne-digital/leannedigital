Shift Wellness Physiotherapy — SEO Report

December 2025

Montly recap

This month was focused on strengthening the core SEO foundation of the site to support sustainable, long-term growth. We made solid progress improving technical SEO, resolving structural issues that were limiting performance and creating a cleaner, more reliable framework for search engines to crawl and understand.

Keyword visibility also moved in the right direction, with early gains across tracked terms, and new content was published to support physiotherapy, pelvic health, nutrition, and injury-related topics. While this content is still early in its lifecycle, it plays an important role in establishing topical authority and reinforcing trust with search engines as it continues to be indexed and evaluated.

Technical SEO

BEFORE
AFTER

This month, we concentrated heavily on improving the site’s technical SEO foundation and resolving issues that were limiting overall performance.

Key results from this work include a significant improvement in the technical site audit score, which has now increased to 82/100. This jump reflects substantial cleanup across the site, including the resolution of a large number of duplicate meta descriptions and title tags, as well as the correction of several 404 and crawl-related issues that were impacting index quality.

We also streamlined the site’s index by excluding low-value utility pages from search results, ensuring search engines are focused only on pages that support rankings and conversions.

From a performance standpoint, these technical improvements are already showing positive momentum:

  • Organic traffic increased month over month

  • 14 new backlinks were acquired, strengthening domain authority and trust signals

  • Crawl efficiency and overall site health improved noticeably

With the technical foundation now much stronger, our goal next month is to push the site into the 90/100 range for technical SEO. Once that benchmark is reached, we can shift into a maintenance phase while focusing more aggressively on sustained keyword growth, content expansion, and local visibility.

Overall, the site is now in a far healthier position structurally, giving future SEO efforts a much stronger platform to build on.

We published a series of new blog posts designed to strengthen topical authority across physiotherapy, pelvic health, nutrition, and injury rehabilitation. The content covers both clinical education and patient-focused guidance, helping support a wider range of search intent while reinforcing key service areas.

The new posts include:

  • Digestive and pelvic health content, focusing on functional constipation and gut wellness, which supports pelvic floor physiotherapy and nutrition-based care.
  • Physiotherapy education content related to motor vehicle accident recovery, helping target high-intent local searches for post-accident treatment.
  • Sports injury content, addressing hamstring injuries in soccer, which supports sports physiotherapy and active rehabilitation services.

Alongside publishing this content, we’ve also begun a structured internal linking campaign. Each blog post will follow a consistent linking formula:

3 internal links to relevant service pages or related educational content

1 high-quality external link to a trusted, authoritative source

This approach has proven highly effective across multiple sites. It helps Google clearly understand site hierarchy, topical relationships, and page importance, while also signaling trust and credibility through authoritative external references. Over time, this strengthens internal structure, improves crawl efficiency, and supports more consistent keyword growth across the site.

Moving forward, this strategy will be applied consistently to all new content to ensure every blog post actively contributes to the site’s overall SEO performance rather than existing in isolation.

Keywords Rankings

BEFORE
AFTER
This month showed strong early progress in search visibility. The site has moved to an average ranking position of 29.49, with 3 keywords now in the Top 3 and 14 ranking in the Top 10. These are encouraging gains after just one month of focused technical, content, and internal linking work. As Google continues to crawl the site and evaluate new content, we expect this steady improvement to continue over the next several months, reinforcing Shift Wellness Physiotherapy as a reliable, well-structured source of information.

Montly Recap

Next Steps
  • Continue improving technical SEO with the goal of reaching and maintaining a high-90s / 100 technical audit score
  • Monitor and support keyword movement as Google processes recent site changes and new content
  • Publish fresh, high-quality content consistently to build authority and depth across key service areas
  • Expand and maintain our structured linking strategy, using internal and external links to reinforce site structure, relevance, and trust signals

This approach keeps the site moving forward while building a strong, scalable SEO foundation for the months ahead.
