Shift Wellness Physiotherapy — SEO Report

February 2026

Montly recap

This month, we took a break from our regular ongoing SEO work and focused only on a small amount of technical maintenance that had already been scheduled. Even with limited activity, the site’s overall SEO health improved slightly, increasing from a 95/100 to a 96/100 score.

We also published four blog posts that were written the previous month, and two of them are already gaining strong traction in Google — Pelvic Floor Dyssynergia During Defecation is generating 26 clicks from 4,697 impressions, while Are Physiotherapists Doctors? has already reached 22 clicks from 12,146 impressions. These early results show strong search visibility and indicate the content is beginning to rank well. We’re looking forward to resuming full SEO efforts in March and continuing to build on this momentum.

Technical SEO

BEFORE
AFTER

During the month, we completed some light technical SEO maintenance to keep the site healthy while regular SEO work was paused. As part of this cleanup, we resolved 11 broken (404) links that were being flagged in audits and could negatively impact crawlability and user experience. We also gained 7 new backlinks, which helps strengthen overall domain authority and supports long-term ranking growth. These improvements contributed to the increase in the site’s SEO score, and once we resume full optimization work next month, we should be able to continue improving this score further.
