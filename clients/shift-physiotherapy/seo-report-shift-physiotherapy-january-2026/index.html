Shift Wellness Physiotherapy — SEO Report

January 2026

Montly recap

This month we saw meaningful progress across both technical SEO and keyword performance, with steady growth reflecting the impact of the work completed to date. Improvements to site structure, index quality, and on-page optimization have strengthened the technical foundation, while new content and internal linking are beginning to translate into better rankings and visibility for core services.

As we pause new content creation in February to focus on releasing existing posts and monitoring performance, it will be important to maintain momentum through continued technical refinements and strategic planning so the gains achieved are preserved and expanded in the months ahead.

Technical SEO

BEFORE
AFTER

This month we focused on strengthening the site’s technical SEO foundation by resolving crawl and on-page issues that were limiting performance. As a result, the site audit score improved from 82 to 95 and total SEO issues were reduced from 86 to 50 through cleanup of duplicate meta descriptions, over-long title tags, and low-value pages.

We refined indexability so search engines prioritize important content, which contributed to organic traffic increasing from 351 to 480 visits. These improvements have created a more efficient, search-friendly structure that positions the site for continued ranking and visibility growth.

This month we published a focused series of educational blog posts centered on pelvic floor health and pessary support to strengthen topical authority in women’s health and specialized pelvic care.

The content addressed common patient questions around how pelvic floor therapy works, when pessaries are recommended, and what to expect from assessment and treatment, helping to capture high-intent searches from individuals seeking non-surgical solutions for prolapse, incontinence, and pelvic pain. Each article was written to support key service pages through structured internal linking while also referencing credible external clinical resources, ensuring the posts contribute directly to rankings, patient trust, and clearer site hierarchy around pelvic floor physiotherapy.

Keywords Rankings

BEFORE
AFTER

This month showed positive movement in overall search visibility, with the site improving from an average position of 29.49 to 25.56 across tracked keywords. We saw 26 keywords move up in rankings, including gains in Top 3 and Top 10 positions, while the number of terms ranking in the Top 100 increased in quality and relevance. Although some fluctuations occurred as new content was indexed and tracked terms were refined, the overall trend reflects stronger keyword alignment with core services such as pelvic floor therapy, physiotherapy, and women’s health.

These improvements indicate that the recent technical SEO work and new content are beginning to translate into measurable ranking growth.

Montly Recap

Next Steps

This month we made strong progress across technical SEO, keyword visibility, and content development, establishing a solid foundation for continued growth. For February, we will pause new content creation and instead focus on gradually releasing the five blog posts already completed, allowing time for each article to be indexed and gain traction.

During this period we will continue monitoring keyword movement, researching emerging search opportunities, and developing a targeted content plan for March. In parallel, we will maintain our technical SEO efforts with the goal of resolving all remaining audit issues and strengthening overall site performance and crawl efficiency.
