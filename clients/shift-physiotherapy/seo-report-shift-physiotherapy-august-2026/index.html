SHIFT PHYSIOTHERAPY

This SEO campaign is focused on improving Shift Physiotherapy’s visibility for local physiotherapy searches and helping more patients find the practice online.

Each month, we monitor technical site health, keyword visibility, and on-page opportunities to strengthen search performance and support sustainable organic growth.

SHIFT PHYSIOTHERAPY — SEO Report

AUGUST 2026

Monthly recap

August reporting shows strong keyword momentum. Across 76 tracked keywords, the average position improved from 14.63 to 12.07 over the last 30 days, a gain of 2.56 positions.

The technical audit remains in excellent condition with a 99/100 on-page SEO score. The latest crawl found just two duplicate meta descriptions, giving us a focused next technical cleanup item.

Technical SEO

The August audit reports a 99/100 on-page SEO score, 652 estimated organic monthly visits, 271 organic keywords, and 180 backlinks.

The crawl reviewed 195 URLs and identified two pages with duplicate meta descriptions. We will review and resolve those descriptions as part of the next technical optimization cycle.

No technical work logged this month.

Keyword rankings

Of 76 tracked keywords, 32 moved up, 22 moved down, and 22 held steady during the reporting period. Visibility remains strong, with 12 keywords in the top 3 and 23 in the top 10.

Average position improved from 13.75 to 12.07, a gain of 1.68 positions. We will continue building on the terms gaining traction while reviewing opportunities among the rankings that softened this month.

Next steps and strategy
