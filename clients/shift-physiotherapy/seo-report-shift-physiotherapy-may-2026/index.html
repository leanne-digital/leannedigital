Shift Wellness Physiotherapy — SEO Report

MAY 2026

Montly recap

This month, keyword rankings continued to move in the right direction as we expanded the site’s content and SEO footprint. We also continued our focus on AI search optimization by adding new FAQ content and structured data designed to help platforms like ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Google’s AI results better understand, reference, and recommend Shift Physiotherapy & Wellness.

Technical SEO

THIS MONTH
LAST MONTH

Technical SEO performance improved to 98/100 this month after resolving a number of minor optimization issues across the site. This included refining SEO title lengths, addressing low-content pages, and noindexing pages with little or no SEO value to help focus crawl budget on the pages that matter most.

The site remains in excellent technical health with no significant issues impacting search visibility or user experience. We’ll continue to monitor the website for crawl errors, broken links, duplicate SEO titles, duplicate meta descriptions, and other technical issues to ensure the site remains clean, efficient, and fully accessible to search engines.

Throughout May, we focused on expanding and strengthening the website’s content and SEO foundation. We published three new physiotherapy articles covering chronic back pain, lower back pain, and runner’s knee recovery to better address common patient questions and concerns.

We also enhanced key pages across the site by adding new FAQ sections and implementing FAQ schema on both the homepage and About page. These updates help improve the site’s structure, provide better information for visitors, and support visibility across search engines and AI-powered platforms.

In addition, we continued strengthening internal linking between service pages and blog content to improve user navigation and reinforce the clinic’s expertise across physiotherapy, wellness, and rehabilitation topics.

Keywords Rankings

THIS MONTH
LAST MONTH
TWO MONTHS AGO

Overall, May was a positive month for SEO performance. We reached a new all time low for average keyword position which improved from 17.47 to 12.57, and we saw more keywords move into the Top 10 results. This indicates that Google is continuing to build confidence in the site and the content strategy we’ve been implementing.

Several keywords experienced significant gains, particularly within physiotherapy, massage therapy, prenatal care, and pelvic health. We also saw strong movement for a number of branded and service-specific terms, helping increase visibility across multiple areas of the practice rather than relying on a single service category.

The new content published this month is helping expand the site’s topical authority and creating additional opportunities for rankings in Edmonton. While some keywords fluctuated, the overall trend remains very positive, with stronger visibility, more first-page rankings, and continued growth across the services that matter most to the clinic.

Next Steps

Next month, we’ll continue building on the momentum we’ve established by publishing new content, expanding FAQ coverage, and implementing additional schema markup across key pages. We’ll also continue refining internal linking and strengthening the site’s overall topical authority in physiotherapy, wellness, and rehabilitation.

We’ll further expand our Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) strategy to improve visibility in AI-powered search platforms such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Google’s AI results. As these platforms continue to grow, ensuring the site is well-structured and easy for AI systems to understand will remain a priority.

At the same time, we’ll continue monitoring technical SEO, fixing any issues that arise, and identifying new opportunities to improve search visibility and user experience across the website.
