Shift Wellness Physiotherapy — SEO Report

November 2025

Montly recap

In November 2025, our first week taking over from the previous SEO agency, we focused heavily on establishing a strong technical foundation for Shift Wellness Physiotherapy. Before beginning any content strategy, we completed a full inspection of the website to ensure there were no conflicts with essential systems such as robots.txt, indexation settings, and Google Search Console connectivity.

Our SEO tools highlighted several technical issues that needed immediate attention. We began by resolving duplicate metadata, improving on-page structures, and bringing the Wix SEO Checklist from 65% to 100% completion. This included optimizing image alt text, fixing titles and descriptions, adding internal links, and ensuring all key pages met Wix’s recommended best practices.

With the technical groundwork stabilized, we moved into content development and created four strategic blog posts designed to target high-intent local search terms. Each article was structured using robust SEO-optimized headings, rich keyword targeting, and a clearly defined hierarchy of H1–H3 tags. We also implemented a combination of internal linking (to strengthen topical authority) and external linking (to credible sources where helpful), improving both crawlability and user experience.

Overall, November’s focus was on cleanup, stabilization, and strategic content creation—laying the foundation for stronger rankings, better topical depth, and long-term organic growth.

Key Improvements Completed

  • Resolved a large number of duplicate meta descriptions and title tags

  • Noindexed low-value utility pages
    Pages such as the Thank-You page were being flagged for low word count and offered no SEO value. These were excluded from indexing to keep the crawl clean and focused on high-value content.
  • Completed the Wix SEO Setup Checklist to 100%

At the start of the month, the site’s built-in Wix SEO Checklist was only 65% complete.
We finished all outstanding tasks, including:

  • Filling in missing titles and meta descriptions
  • Adding internal links
  • Adding alt text to all images
  • Optimizing on-page SEO signals across key service pages
    Completing this checklist ensures Wix’s internal SEO tools recognize the site as fully optimized.

Improved On-Page SEO Score (60 → 71)
This reflects the technical clean-up work completed, giving search engines a clearer and more consistent structure to crawl.

Impact

These corrections significantly improved the site’s technical health, reduced duplicate-content warnings, and ensured only the right pages are eligible for ranking. With a cleaner index and a fully completed Wix SEO checklist, the website is now in a much stronger position for ongoing keyword growth and local visibility.

This month, we focused on producing high-quality, search-optimized content designed to strengthen topical authority across key service areas such as pelvic health, sports rehabilitation, and general physiotherapy education. Our goal was to publish content that not only ranks well, but also supports the clinic’s real-world expertise and improves user engagement.

We created four fully optimized blog posts, each built with robust SEO-driven heading structures (H1–H3), keyword-dense subtopics, and clear educational value. These articles target high-intent queries such as shoulder pain rehab, hamstring injuries, bowel-related pelvic floor dysfunction, and consumer questions around physiotherapy credentials. All content was crafted to match the clinic’s professional tone while still providing accessible, patient-friendly information.

In addition to publishing new content, we also implemented strategic internal linking to reinforce topical clusters. Each new article links out to relevant older posts such as Understanding Muscle Cramps, The Truth About Back Pain, and Leaking Urine? Pelvic Floor Physiotherapy Might Be Right for You. This strengthens site structure, improves crawl depth, and helps search engines better understand the relationships between topics—ultimately improving rankings over time.

Overall, November marked a strong foundation for long-term content growth. With four new optimized articles and improved internal linking, the blog is now better positioned to capture local search traffic in Edmonton and expand visibility across core physiotherapy and pelvic-health topics.

Keywords Rankings

While rankings are moving in the right direction, we still have work ahead to bring the average position into the 20s and push more keywords onto the first page of Google. The encouraging news is that our early efforts are already showing positive momentum, with several keywords experiencing strong upward movement this month. As we continue refining technical SEO, expanding content, and strengthening internal linking, we fully expect to see even greater improvements in the coming months.

Structured Content for Stronger SEO

To maximize search visibility and improve how Google understands each article, all blog posts this month were built using a proper SEO-focused heading hierarchy. Each post includes a single, clear H1 title, followed by well-organized H2 and H3 subheadings that break the content into logical, keyword-rich sections.

This structure not only helps readers navigate the information easily, but also signals topical relevance to search engines. By incorporating multiple descriptive, keyword-optimized headings throughout each article, we ensure your content is both highly scannable for users and fully aligned with modern SEO best practices.

Montly Recap

November marked a strong start to our partnership, with a focus on rebuilding the technical foundation of the website and improving overall search performance. We resolved several structural issues flagged by our SEO tools, completed the Wix SEO Checklist to 100%, cleaned up duplicate metadata, and noindexed low-value pages to create a cleaner crawl path for Google.

On the content side, we produced four fully optimized, keyword-rich blog posts targeting high-intent search terms across physiotherapy, pelvic health, and sports rehab. These were written with proper SEO hierarchy (H1, H2, H3) and supported by strategic internal linking to strengthen topical authority. With both technical and content improvements implemented, Shift Wellness Physiotherapy is now positioned for stronger organic growth heading into December.

Next Steps

As we move into the upcoming month, we’ll continue building on this momentum with a balanced approach across technical SEO, content expansion, and ongoing evaluation. Our next steps include:

  • Publishing additional strategic, SEO-optimized blog content to target more local and high-intent keywords.
  • Fixing remaining technical SEO items, including metadata refinements, crawl efficiency improvements, and ongoing audit cleanup.
  • Expanding internal linking between relevant service pages and blog posts to enhance topical clusters and improve page authority.
  • Monitoring performance metrics such as traffic, keyword growth, and page engagement to ensure continuous improvement.

With these efforts, we’ll continue strengthening both visibility and ranking stability, setting Shift Wellness up for long-term organic success.
