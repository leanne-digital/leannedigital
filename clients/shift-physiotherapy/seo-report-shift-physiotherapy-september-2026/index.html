Google Ads

As we take over management of Shift’s Google Ads account, our first priority will be improving the quality of the data Google is using to optimize the campaigns. The account is currently tracking a number of different actions as conversions, so we’ll begin by separating true lead and booking activity from lower-value engagement actions. From there, we’ll refine campaign structure, search terms, targeting, scheduling and landing-page alignment so budget is focused more heavily on the searches and services that are most likely to generate actual appointments.

This first month of management will be focused on getting the account’s foundation right before making larger changes to bidding or budgets. Once conversion tracking is cleaned up and we have a clearer picture of which campaigns and searches are producing actual bookings, we’ll be able to make more informed decisions about automated bidding, budget allocation and where additional advertising spend can generate the strongest return.

Next steps and strategy

In October, we’ll continue adding fresh, optimized content and expand the internal linking strategy across more pages and blog posts. We’ll also begin cleaning up Google Tag Manager and the Google Ads account so conversion tracking, campaign data and future optimizations are built on a cleaner and more reliable foundation.
