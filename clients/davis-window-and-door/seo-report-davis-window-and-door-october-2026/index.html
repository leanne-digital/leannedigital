Monthly recap

October 2026

October focused on launching the redesigned Windows page and making it easier for customers to request a consultation. We implemented the approved Atlanta-focused content, corrected the starting price to $2,000, refined the layouts across devices and improved the product images and search metadata. The approved page is now live at its existing address.

We also repaired website email sending, improved the contact form and confirmation message, and upgraded the site to PHP 8.3. Review rounds with Fee helped bring the page through approval and launch.

Technical SEO

Download October technical SEO audit (PDF)

Davis achieved a 100/100 on-page SEO score in the October 27 audit, with zero SEO issues reported and no broken pages. The long page title flagged in the earlier audit is no longer listed as an issue.
