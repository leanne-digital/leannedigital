Davis Window & Door

AEO & Technical SEO Report

October 2026

Monthly recap

October work to date focused on improving the Windows landing page, strengthening its SEO metadata and product imagery, and repairing the consultation form experience. Following stakeholder review and approval, the redesigned Windows page was published at its existing URL. Supporting work included responsive layout and call-to-action refinements, descriptive image alt text, WebP conversion, an audit word-count investigation, and post-launch cache and publication checks.

The contact form was also updated with dedicated SMTP sending, cleaner notification routing, clearer required fields and a more readable submission confirmation.

Technical SEO

The October 20 audit recorded a 100/100 on-page SEO score, no broken pages, and one issue: a page title that was too long. This audit predates the October 25–26 work listed below and does not verify those later changes.
