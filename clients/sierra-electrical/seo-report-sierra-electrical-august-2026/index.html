SIERRA ELECTRICAL LTD. — SEO Report

AUGUST 2026

Monthly recap

August delivered a clean technical result and meaningful ranking progress. The site maintained a perfect 100/100 on-page SEO score with no issues identified in the latest audit.

Keyword visibility improved from an average position of 15.46 to 12.11 across the tracked set, strengthening the campaign’s foundation for continued local search growth.

We also created a dedicated Careers page and published a Google Business Profile post to support the search for a new apprentice. Next, we will look for additional no-cost ways to increase reach, including relevant Facebook and Kijiji opportunities.

Technical SEO

Sierra Electrical’s August technical audit achieved a 100/100 on-page SEO score, with 162 estimated organic monthly visits, 50 organic keywords, and 286 backlinks.

The crawl reviewed 72 URLs and found zero SEO issues. With 49 successful pages, 22 redirects, no broken URLs, and one blocked page, the site is in excellent technical shape for crawling and indexing.

Keyword rankings

Of 38 tracked keywords, 22 moved up, nine moved down, and seven held steady. Average position improved from 15.46 to 12.11, a gain of 3.35 positions.

The strongest movement was in top-10 visibility, which increased from one keyword to four. The campaign now has 10 keywords in the top 3, 24 in the top 100, and no keywords outside the top 100.

Google Ads

August Google Ads generated 22 conversions from $2,394.65 in spend. After shifting the campaign toward a commercial-heavy budget allocation, conversion volume declined by four compared with the previous period.

The current campaign data reinforces why the new strategy needs close monitoring: residential generated 19 conversions from $1,261.34 in spend, while commercial generated three conversions from $1,144.24. That places the current commercial cost per conversion at roughly 5.7 times the residential level.

We will continue testing the commercial-heavy approach, but if commercial lead volume and quality do not justify the added acquisition cost, we should consider returning more budget to the established residential campaign.

Next steps and strategy

August closes with a perfect technical audit and a 3.35-position improvement in average rankings, creating a strong platform for the next phase of SEO work.

Next month, we will protect that technical baseline while expanding the on-page, internal-linking, and local-content work that supports further top-10 keyword growth.

We will also monitor the commercial-heavy Google Ads strategy closely and develop additional low-cost recruitment promotion for the Careers page, including suitable Facebook and Kijiji listings if they fit the role and audience.
