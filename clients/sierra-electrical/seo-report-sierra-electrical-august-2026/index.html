Monthly Recap

Next steps and strategy

This month’s work focused on making Google Ads conversion data reliable. Phone-click conversions were firing inconsistently because the trigger depended on a GCLID cookie. We audited the conversion ID and label, removed that cookie requirement, limited tracking to the Residential and Commercial quote landing pages, and confirmed the tag in Google Tag Manager Preview.

Unrelated Zapier tags were paused while we isolated the Google Ads setup. With conversions now firing on phone clicks, we also shifted the campaign weighting to a 70/30 commercial vs residential split so more of the budget supports commercial lead generation.

We also launched a Careers page for the new job posting, published a Google Business Profile post to promote the role, created a Compressed Air Piping service page, and added that service and description to the Google Business Profile.
