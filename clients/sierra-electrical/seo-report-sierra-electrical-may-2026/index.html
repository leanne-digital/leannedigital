Google Ads

Google Ads continues to perform well as we refine the campaign strategy and gradually shift more budget toward commercial electrical services. While conversions were slightly lower this month, this is expected as commercial leads are typically more competitive and harder to acquire than residential leads, often involving larger projects and longer sales cycles. Despite the shift, the campaigns generated 163 clicks, 3,690 impressions, and 23 tracked conversions over the past 30 days, while continuing to increase Sierra Electrical’s visibility across both residential and commercial markets.

Overall, the campaigns remain on track and are helping position the company for higher-value commercial opportunities while maintaining a steady flow of residential inquiries.

Montly Recap

Next Steps and strategy

As we move into June, we’ll continue executing the same strategy that has produced strong results over the past several months. Our focus will remain on expanding location and commercial-focused content, strengthening internal linking, optimizing existing pages, and identifying new opportunities through competitor analysis.

We’ll also be placing greater emphasis on AI and LLM visibility by creating content and resources that are more likely to be referenced by platforms such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Google’s AI-powered search results. On the advertising side, we’ll maintain the current residential and commercial campaign structure, continuing to build momentum in the commercial market while generating a steady flow of residential leads.
