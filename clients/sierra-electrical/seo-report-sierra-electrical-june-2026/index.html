SIERRA ELECTRICAL LTD.

This SEO campaign is focused on increasing Sierra Electrical Ltd.’s visibility across Edmonton and surrounding areas to attract more commercial electrical projects.

Each month, we strengthen the website’s technical performance, optimize service pages for high-value commercial keywords, and expand targeted content aimed at property managers, builders, and business owners searching for reliable electrical contractors.

Ongoing work includes improving site structure, enhancing metadata, building strategic internal links, and publishing industry-focused content designed to generate qualified commercial leads. The goal is to improve search rankings, increase inbound inquiries, and position Sierra Electrical as a trusted commercial electrical contractor in the Edmonton market.

Since taking over the monthly ongoing SEO campaign on March 1st, Sierra Electrical has shown strong and consistent ranking growth in Google Search Console. This month’s content work focused on refreshing older blog content, improving the main Commercial Electrician in Edmonton service page, and using Google Search Console data to guide keyword targeting. We reviewed ranking and impression data to identify which commercial and Edmonton service terms were gaining visibility, then used those insights to refine page headings, service copy, internal linking, and supporting blog content.

Several older commercial-related blog posts were also reviewed and optimized to better support the main commercial services page. By cleaning up redundant service wording, strengthening commercial electrician keyword relevance, and improving the structure of the page, we helped make the content clearer for users while giving Google a stronger signal about Sierra Electrical’s commercial electrical services in Edmonton.

Google Ads

Google Ads continued to generate steady lead activity this month, producing 29 tracked conversions over the past 30 days, including form submissions and calls directly from ads. This works out to roughly one conversion per day, which is a positive result considering the campaign is now more heavily focused on commercial electrical services, where leads are typically more competitive and higher-value than standard residential inquiries.

The campaign also generated 167 clicks and approximately 3.6K impressions over the past 30 days, with the week of June 15th producing 47 clicks, one of the stronger traffic weeks in the reporting period. Additional phone calls made directly from the landing page are not included in this conversion total, so overall lead activity may be higher than what is shown inside Google Ads.

Montly Recap

Next Steps and strategy

SEO continued to move in the right direction, with technical health holding at 100/100 and keyword visibility improving. We also updated the Commercial Electrician in Edmonton page and refreshed supporting commercial blog content.

Next month, we’ll keep improving service pages, internal links, and new page opportunities from Search Console data. Google Ads will remain focused on commercial growth, especially after a strong week last week with increased click activity.
