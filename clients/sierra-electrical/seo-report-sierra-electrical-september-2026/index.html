Monthly recap

September 2026

September focused on strengthening Sierra Electrical’s overall search visibility through a combination of technical SEO, AEO, internal linking, and Google Ads improvements. We expanded structured data across key service and location pages, improved internal links between related services and supporting content, cleaned up on-page SEO issues, and reviewed Google Ads performance as the budget shifted more heavily toward Commercial campaigns.

Technical SEO

Sierra Electrical maintained its perfect 100/100 technical SEO audit score in September, with no SEO issues or broken pages and links detected. The audit recorded 22 redirected URLs, unchanged from August. The site continues to provide a clean, well-maintained technical foundation, and regular monitoring and maintenance will help keep it that way as new content and updates are added.

Google Ads

September reflected the requested shift toward Commercial advertising, with approximately 70% of Google Ads spend allocated to Commercial campaigns and 30% to Residential. Commercial visibility increased, generating 75 clicks during the month, but the campaign did not record a conversion. Residential continued to be the strongest source of leads, generating 34 conversions from only $730 in spend. Overall account conversions increased from 22 in August to 34 in September despite a slight decline in clicks.

Based on this performance, we recommend continuing to prioritize Commercial growth while treating the 70/30 allocation as a target rather than a strict spending requirement. This will allow us to maintain Residential lead volume while we improve Commercial keywords, ads, landing pages and conversion efficiency.

Next steps and strategy

Next month, we’ll place more emphasis on Google Business Profile optimization and continue expanding Sierra Electrical’s AEO strategy. This will include improving local business signals, service and location information, and answer-focused content that can be more easily understood by Google’s evolving AI-powered search experiences. We’ll also continue monitoring Google Ads performance and work on improving Commercial campaign efficiency while maintaining the strong lead volume coming from Residential.
