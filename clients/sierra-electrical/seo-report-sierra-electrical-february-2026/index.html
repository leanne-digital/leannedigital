FEBRUARY 2026

Montly recap

This month we focused on continued growth and optimization across both SEO and paid marketing. We published new SEO-focused content, maintained and monitored the Google Ads campaigns along with conversion tracking metrics, and strengthened the site structure by adding over 50 new internal links to improve crawlability and topical authority. We also optimized Google Search Console settings to ensure proper indexing and site health.

As a result of these combined efforts, keyword rankings improved significantly — with average positions moving from 46.87 to 29.33, representing roughly a 37% improvement in rankings month over month — a strong indicator that overall visibility and search performance are moving in the right direction.

Technical SEO

This month we focused on strengthening the website’s technical SEO foundation and improving how search engines crawl and index the site. We resolved a number of 404 errors and added proper redirects to ensure visitors and search engines are directed to the correct pages. Using Google Search Console, we identified pages that were not being indexed and implemented redirects and fixes where needed to improve crawl efficiency.

We also optimized SEO titles and meta descriptions across key pages to improve relevance and search visibility, and submitted the website sitemap to Google Search Console to help Google discover and index content more effectively.

This month we focused heavily on strengthening internal linking across the website. Internal links help Google better understand how pages relate to one another and signal that the site is well organized, authoritative, and valuable for users. By strategically connecting service pages, blog content, and core site pages, we are improving crawlability and helping search engines discover and prioritize important content for indexing. This structured linking approach supports stronger rankings over time by reinforcing topic relevance and overall site authority.

Keywords Rankings

This month we saw a strong improvement in keyword performance, with rankings moving up an average of 17 positions, which is a very positive indicator that our SEO efforts are gaining traction and moving in the right direction. Several high-value search terms with meaningful search volume are beginning to climb into competitive positions, including “electrician edmonton” (1,900 monthly searches), “edmonton electricians” (1,000 monthly searches), and “residential electrician edmonton” (260 monthly searches).

These gains show that Google is increasingly recognizing the site’s authority and relevance within the Edmonton electrical services market, and continued optimization and internal linking should help push more of these keywords into top-page rankings.
