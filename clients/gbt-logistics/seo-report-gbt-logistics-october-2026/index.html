GBT Logistics & Packaging Inc.

Monthly recap

October 2026

October focused on strengthening GBT’s website and local search presence. We shortened 125 page titles, resolved a 22-page duplicate-title group, added two missing descriptions and refined the privacy-policy and sitemap titles. We also completed the Search Console review, set up Bing Places and secured Google Business Profile access.

We added and verified 25 internal links across 21 pages, helping visitors find relevant services. The latest audit shows a 100/100 SEO score with no SEO issues detected. In November, we’ll build on this foundation with more internal linking, local profile improvements and continued website checks.

Technical SEO

GBT achieved a 100/100 SEO audit score following the website cleanup. We shortened lengthy titles, replaced duplicate titles and filled in missing descriptions. The latest audit, dated November 1, checked 293 pages and reported no SEO issues, broken pages or blocked pages.
