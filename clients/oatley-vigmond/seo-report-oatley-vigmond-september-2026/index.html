OATLEY VIGMOND — SEO Report

SEPTEMBER 2026

Monthly recap

September work to date focused on correcting search-result metadata and a broken event URL. We resolved duplicate titles on five pages, shortened 19 overlong title tags, added unique meta descriptions to 21 accident and injury archives, and redirected one missing event-date page to the main events listing.

We also investigated mobile performance and Core Web Vitals issues, repaired the WP Rocket installation, removed overlapping optimization from SiteGround Speed Optimizer, and improved CSS and JavaScript delivery. Homepage mobile re-tests improved from roughly 50 to 90–96, with LCP at about 2.3–3.2 seconds and Total Blocking Time at 0 ms.

This report records September work to date, including the SEO updates verified on September 14 and the performance fixes and re-test results supplied for this report. September charts and full reporting exports will be added when supplied. Changes in rankings, traffic, inquiries and the audit score remain to be assessed.

Technical SEO

Completed: 24 SEO title updates, 21 new meta descriptions and one 301 redirect. The detailed log below lists every affected URL and its final title or description.

Mobile performance work included a fresh WP Rocket installation, resolution of the previous installation’s critical error, disabling SiteGround Speed Optimizer, and CSS/JavaScript minification, deferred and delayed JavaScript, and Remove Unused CSS. Reported homepage mobile re-tests improved from roughly 50 to 90–96; LCP fell to about 2.3–3.2 seconds and Total Blocking Time to 0 ms. These test results do not by themselves confirm a pass for site-wide field Core Web Vitals.

The August audit is shown as last month’s reference. September’s technical audit chart and updated score are pending. A fresh crawl is needed to confirm the audit tool has cleared the reported issues.
