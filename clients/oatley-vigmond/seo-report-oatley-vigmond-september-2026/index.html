Monthly recap

September 2026

September focused on strengthening Oatley Vigmond’s technical SEO foundation and improving site performance. We corrected metadata issues across key pages, resolved a broken event URL, repaired the site’s performance optimization setup and significantly improved mobile speed scores. This work gives us a cleaner technical base to build on as we shift more attention toward AEO, structured data and AI search readiness in October.

Technical SEO

Completed: 24 SEO title updates, 21 new meta descriptions and one 301 redirect. The detailed log below lists every affected URL and its final title or description.

Mobile performance work included a fresh WP Rocket installation, resolution of the previous installation’s critical error, disabling SiteGround Speed Optimizer, and CSS/JavaScript minification, deferred and delayed JavaScript, and Remove Unused CSS. Reported homepage mobile re-tests improved from roughly 50 to 90–96; LCP fell to about 2.3–3.2 seconds and Total Blocking Time to 0 ms. These test results do not by themselves confirm a pass for site-wide field Core Web Vitals.

The August audit is shown as last month’s reference. The September 26 audit is shown above for this month.

Next steps and strategy

In October, we’ll complete a fresh technical crawl to confirm the September fixes have cleared, then focus on AEO and AI search readiness.
