OATLEY VIGMOND ONGOING MONTHLY SEO/GEO/AEO (3hrs)

This SEO growth initiative is focused on strengthening Oatley Vigmond’s visibility across Google Search, Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Gemini, and other emerging AI-powered search platforms. Our work extends beyond traditional SEO to include technical SEO, AI search optimization (AEO), geographic search optimization (GEO), content architecture, conversion tracking, and marketing automation.

Each month, we improve site structure, resolve technical issues, enhance internal linking, optimize practice area and injury-related content, expand authoritative legal resources, and strengthen the firm’s presence across both traditional and AI-driven search experiences. We also manage and monitor key systems including Google Ads, Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics 4, Call Tracking Metrics, Lead Docket, Zapier automations, and conversion attribution to ensure marketing performance is measured accurately.

Our ongoing strategy includes technical SEO improvements, AI search optimization, local visibility enhancements, content hub development, schema implementation where appropriate, competitor analysis, and conversion rate optimization. The goal is simple: increase qualified case inquiries, improve visibility for high-value personal injury searches across Ontario, and further establish Oatley Vigmond as one of Canada’s leading personal injury law firms.

Montly Recap

During May, we focused heavily on technical SEO, AI search optimization, and conversion tracking accuracy. Technical improvements included resolving broken links, correcting missing H1 tags across 21 pages within the Catastrophic Injury Family Support Hub, and refining page structure to improve crawlability and content organization. We also expanded FAQ coverage and deployed additional FAQ JSON-LD schema across key practice area and expertise pages to strengthen content understanding for both search engines and AI-powered search platforms.

On the conversion tracking side, we completed a comprehensive audit of the tracking ecosystem, including Google Ads, Bing Ads, Reddit Ads, Meta Ads, Google Tag Manager, GA4, Lead Docket, and Ngage integrations. Several attribution and lead-source tracking issues were identified and corrected, helping ensure leads are being properly categorized and attributed across marketing channels. We also developed a dedicated Zapier Conversion Tracking Audit tool to independently validate conversion data against Lead Docket records and provide additional reporting transparency.

Next Steps

At our June 1 strategy meeting, we should discuss:

• Expanding the Injury Claim landing page strategy and supporting content
• Increasing advertising budgets where conversion quality justifies additional investment
• Additional practice area FAQ development and FAQ JSON-LD implementation
• Expanding Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) initiatives for ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Google AI results
• Additional verdict, settlement, and testimonial optimization opportunities
• Continued improvement of conversion tracking and attribution reporting
• Additional internal linking opportunities across practice area and expertise content
• Expansion of AI search visibility monitoring and reporting
• Opportunities to improve Lead Docket source attribution and reporting accuracy
• New content initiatives targeting high-value personal injury and catastrophic injury topics across Ontario
• Further technical SEO improvements and site architecture enhancements
• Ongoing monitoring of indexing, crawlability, metadata quality, and technical SEO health
• Explore unique content (verdicts and settlements)

These initiatives will help determine priorities for June and whether increasing the marketing budget would allow us to accelerate growth opportunities identified during this month’s audits and optimization work.
