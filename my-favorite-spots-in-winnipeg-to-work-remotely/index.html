My Favourite Spots in Winnipeg to Work Remotely

Leanne Jones October 9, 2025

Key Takeaways

  • The Forks is the perfect mix of focus and fun — grab a cinnamon bun, find a seat upstairs, and take a quick arcade break when you need to reset.
  • Starbucks is reliable, social, and great for casual networking — sometimes showing up with your laptop opens unexpected doors.
  • IKEA is a dream for parents — free childcare, strong coffee, and plenty of outlets make it a guilt-free work haven.
  • Scout Coffee is cozy and kid-friendly — ideal when you want to work while your little ones play.
  • Winnipeg has no shortage of creative work spots — you just have to find the one that fits your mood that day.
