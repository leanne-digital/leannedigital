Winnipeg Indigenous Graphic Design

Design is more than visuals, it is storytelling. As a Indigenous Graphic Designer with Roots from Peguis First Nation, I create meaningful designs that celebrate culture, connect with community, and help businesses share their story with the world.

Storytelling Through Design

Every design tells a story. As an Indigenous Graphic Designer, I draw inspiration from the teachings, resilience, and creativity of my community. For me, visuals are not just about looking good, they are about carrying meaning, sharing histories, and creating connections. Whether it is a logo, a website, or a digital asset, I focus on designs that honor the story behind the business while building trust with its audience.

Leanne Jones - Indigenous Graphic Designer

I grew up in Selkirk and I am a proud member of Peguis First Nation. I am also a mom of two boys, and everything I do is guided by my passion for helping my community. Design has always been my way of creating connection and telling stories, and I believe in using my skills to uplift others.

I earned diplomas in both Graphic Design and Digital Multimedia from Red River College and built over a decade of experience working in agencies and in-house design teams. In 2023, I opened Leanne Digital to combine my professional expertise with my values. My goal is to create meaningful, story-driven design that helps Indigenous businesses and organizations grow while staying true to their culture.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Leanne Digital based?

Leanne Digital is an Indigenous-owned digital marketing agency based in Winnipeg, Manitoba, on Treaty 1 territory. We work with clients across Canada and beyond.

What services does Leanne Digital offer?

We offer website design, website management and support, SEO, answer engine optimization (AEO), graphic design, logo design, Indigenous web and graphic design, and Google Ads.

How do I get started?

The easiest way is to book a call or send a message on our contact page. Tell us what you are working on and we will help you figure out the best next step.

Do you work with clients outside Winnipeg?

Yes. While we are based in Winnipeg, we regularly work with businesses and Indigenous organizations across Canada and remotely.
