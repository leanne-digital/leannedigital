About Leanne Digital

Leanne Digital, formerly Leanne Digital Design, is an Indigenous-owned digital marketing and branding agency based in Winnipeg, Manitoba. We help small to medium-sized businesses and Indigenous organizations build strong brands, professional websites, and digital marketing systems that help them get seen, build trust, and grow online.

LET's CHAT!

How It Started

I'm Leanne, founder and creative director of Leanne Digital. With over 10 years of experience in branding, web design, and digital marketing, I've worked in both agency and in house environments before launching the studio in 2023.

I built Leanne Digital because I wanted to bring together the two things I care about most in this work: beautiful design and real strategy. A brand should look good, but it also needs to help people understand who you are, trust what you do, and choose you.

That's why our work connects brand clarity, website design, SEO, paid ads, and digital strategy. We create brands and websites that feel professional, tell the right story, and help businesses get seen online.

As a proud member of Peguis First Nation and an Indigenous woman in tech, I'm especially passionate about supporting Indigenous-owned businesses and organizations. My goal is to create marketing and visual systems that honour culture, community, and story while helping businesses grow with confidence.

Indigenous-Owned & Certified

Leanne Digital is a Certified Indigenous Business through the Canadian Council for Indigenous Business (CCIB). We are proud to be Indigenous-owned and operated, with our identities and values rooted in community.

  • Leanne Jones – Founder and Creative Director – is of Peguis First Nation in Manitoba.
  • Gary Burns – CTO, Web and Technology Expert – is of Kahnawake reserve near Montréal.

Together, we bring creative and technical expertise while honoring our Indigenous heritage. Supporting Indigenous entrepreneurship and cultural integrity is at the heart of the work we do.
