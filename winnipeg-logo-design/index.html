Frequently Asked Questions

What makes a good logo?

Simple, memorable, flexible, and aligned with your brand personality.

How long does it take?

Most logo projects take about two to three weeks depending on revisions.

What files do I receive?

PNG, JPG, vector files, layout variations, and a simple brand guide.

Do you offer full branding?

Yes. Full identity packages are available if you want a complete and cohesive brand system.

Do you offer “Just The Logo” as a package?

Yes I do! While I recommend most businesses need a full branding strategy I understand some businesses are just getting their footing and literally just need the logo.

Do You just service Winnipeg for Logo Design?
I am proudly Winnipeg based, but you do not need to live here to get a great logo from me. If you have WiFi, we can work together.
