Our Key Findings

1

SEO Titles & Meta Descriptions

Several pages are currently using generic titles, including:

  • Home - WordPress
  • About - WordPress
  • E-2 Visa - WordPress

These titles don't clearly communicate your services or take full advantage of relevant search terms.

The audit also identified 13 pages missing meta descriptions.

We recommend updating these elements so your pages better communicate their purpose to search engines and potential visitors.

2

Website Content & Keyword Targeting

Some pages have relatively limited content, including your FAQ and portfolio pages.

We also identified opportunities to better target searches related to:

  • Franchise consulting services
  • Franchise brokers
  • Franchise ownership opportunities
  • E-2 Visa franchise opportunities
  • Franchise investment guidance

The goal is to make sure your website answers the questions prospective franchise buyers are asking and provides clear information about how you can help.

3

Technical SEO

The audit identified technical items that warrant further review, including indexing settings, website titles and URL structure.

We'd also review your sitemap, internal linking, structured data and overall website organization to make sure search engines can properly access and understand your content.

4

Website Design & Lead Generation

Your website should reflect the expertise and personal guidance you provide as a franchise consultant.

We'd recommend improving the overall visual design, strengthening your messaging and making it easier for visitors to understand your process and book a consultation.

Our Recommendation

Based on our discussion, we'd recommend Option A: WordPress Business Website.

Since you're already familiar with WordPress and Elementor, it makes sense to continue using a platform that allows you to manage your own blog posts and website content.

The SEO issues identified in our audit can also be addressed within WordPress, meaning you don't need to change platforms to improve your search visibility.

However, if you're interested in exploring AI-assisted website management and prefer having our team handle technical updates, the React option is worth considering.

Both options would provide Lone Peak Franchise Group with a professionally designed website and a stronger foundation for future SEO efforts.
