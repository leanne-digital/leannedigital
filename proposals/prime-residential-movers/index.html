Project Overview

Thank you for taking the time to meet with me, Leo. It was great learning more about Prime Residential Movers and your plans for the website.

During our conversation, you mentioned that you've invested significantly in website development and SEO over the years. While your website has generated organic traffic and leads, you're experiencing challenges with its performance, outdated design, and limitations with Beaver Builder and your current hosting provider.

You're also actively learning more about SEO and AI search visibility, and want the flexibility to implement those strategies yourself without relying on a developer for every update.

Our recommendation is to rebuild your existing website in WordPress using Elementor, retaining the content and SEO work you've already invested in while creating a faster, more modern website that's easier to manage.

The goal isn't to start over. It's to improve the foundation of what you've already built.

Our Recommended Approach

After reviewing your existing website, I identified approximately 35 pages across your core website, moving services, and service locations, in addition to your existing blog posts.

Because of the size of your website and the SEO work already completed, I recommend rebuilding the entire website rather than reducing its content or starting with a smaller site.

To make the process more cost-effective, we'll develop reusable Elementor templates for your service and location pages. These templates will allow us to rebuild your existing pages efficiently while maintaining a consistent design throughout the website.

They will also make it easier for you to create additional pages in the future.

01

Website Design & Development

We'll rebuild Prime Residential Movers in WordPress using Elementor, replacing the existing Beaver Builder setup.

Website Design

  • Updated homepage design with a more modern, professional appearance
  • Improved website navigation and content organization
  • Consistent styling, typography, and page layouts
  • Clear calls to action encouraging visitors to request moving quotes
  • Responsive design for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices

Website Development

  • WordPress and Elementor setup
  • Rebuilding approximately 35 existing website pages
  • Transferring existing text, images, and content
  • Reusable Elementor templates for moving service pages
  • Reusable Elementor templates for location-specific pages
  • Blog post template and migration of existing blog posts
  • New website inquiry form
  • Integration of essential website functionality
  • Cross-browser and mobile testing

All existing pages will be rebuilt using their current content. This proposal does not include rewriting the content or developing new SEO landing pages.

Designed for Future Growth

One of the benefits of Elementor is that you'll be able to manage and expand the website yourself.

For example, if you want to create a new location page targeting an additional Florida community, you can duplicate an existing template, update the content, and publish the page without needing a developer to create a new layout.

This gives you more control over your ongoing SEO efforts while maintaining a consistent website design.

02

SEO Preservation & Technical Setup

You've already invested considerable resources into SEO, and preserving that work is an important part of this rebuild.

We'll take care to retain your existing content and website structure wherever possible.

What's Included

  • Review of existing page URLs and website structure
  • Preservation of existing URLs wherever possible
  • Transfer of existing page titles and meta descriptions where appropriate
  • Migration of existing blog posts
  • Preservation of relevant internal links
  • Redirects where URL changes are necessary
  • XML sitemap setup
  • Search engine indexing configuration
  • Foundational technical SEO optimization
  • Mobile responsiveness and performance-focused development
  • Website testing before launch

A website rebuild can sometimes cause temporary changes in search rankings as search engines recrawl the updated pages. Our approach is designed to minimize unnecessary disruption by retaining the existing content and URLs wherever possible.

Supporting Your SEO & AI Search Goals

During our call, you mentioned that customers have been discovering Prime Residential Movers through ChatGPT, which is an encouraging sign.

We'll structure the rebuilt website to support your continued SEO efforts, including:

  • Service-specific content
  • Location-specific landing pages
  • Internal linking between relevant services and locations
  • Clear page headings and content organization
  • Search-engine-friendly website architecture

This will provide a foundation for you to continue implementing the SEO strategies you're learning.

Ongoing SEO campaigns, keyword research, new content writing, and AI search optimization services are not included in the initial rebuild, but can be added separately if needed.
