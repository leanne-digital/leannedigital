Project Overview

DCD Canada is a new national initiative being developed by a dedicated group of parents, researchers, clinicians, and advocates who want to improve awareness, understanding, and support for people living with Developmental Coordination Disorder (DCD).

Although DCD is estimated to be as common as ADHD, awareness remains limited, and families often struggle to find reliable information, resources, and support.

DCD Canada aims to address these gaps by bringing resources together, supporting individuals and families across all stages of life, and advocating for improved recognition and access to services throughout Canada.

As the organization works toward obtaining charitable status, establishing an online presence is an important first step.

Leanne Digital recommends starting with a professionally designed one-page website and a simple, consistent visual identity. This will give DCD Canada a place to introduce its mission, connect with interested individuals, and begin building awareness while the organization develops.

Our Recommended Approach

Rather than investing in a larger website immediately, we recommend creating a simple, flexible website that can expand as DCD Canada grows.

The initial website will serve as an introduction to the organization, with clear information about its purpose, current activities, and future plans.

Our approach will focus on accessibility, readability, and a visual identity that feels professional without being overly clinical or formal.

We also recommend building the website using WordPress and Elementor. This will allow committee members to make simple content updates themselves and provide a foundation for additional pages and resources in the future.

Scope of Work

01

Website Design & Development

A custom-designed, one-page WordPress website built to establish DCD Canada's online presence.

Proposed website sections

Introduction
Introduce DCD Canada and communicate that a new national organization is being established.
About DCD
A brief explanation of Developmental Coordination Disorder and why greater awareness is needed.
Mission & Vision
Outline the organization's purpose and long-term goals.
Resources
Provide links to trusted DCD information and research, including resources such as CanChild.
Get Involved
Invite individuals, families, clinicians, researchers, and other interested parties to connect with the organization.
Contact
A simple contact form and links to social media profiles.

Website features include

  • Custom homepage design
  • WordPress and Elementor development
  • Mobile and tablet responsive layouts
  • Accessible typography, colours, and page structure
  • Basic on-page SEO and search engine setup
  • Contact form integration
  • Social media links
  • Website performance and security basics
  • Domain connection and website launch
  • Basic WordPress training
  • 30 days of post-launch support for issues related to the completed website

The website will be structured to accommodate future additions, such as expanded educational resources, news, events, and fundraising opportunities.

02

Logo Refinement & Visual Identity

DCD Canada has already begun exploring logo concepts, including designs featuring the blue-footed booby and the teal colour associated with DCD awareness.

We will use these existing concepts as a starting point, refining the committee's preferred direction into a consistent visual identity.

Included

  • Review of existing logo concepts
  • Refinement of one selected logo direction
  • Typography and colour adjustments
  • Final digital logo files
  • Colour palette and basic font selections

The goal is to create a recognizable identity that works well across the website and social media, while reflecting DCD Canada's role as a national organization serving people of all ages.

This service includes refinement of an existing logo concept rather than a complete brand identity development process.

03

Social Media Launch Graphics

To help establish DCD Canada's presence across social platforms, we will prepare basic branded assets for:

  • Facebook
  • Instagram
  • LinkedIn

This includes appropriately sized profile imagery and cover graphics where applicable, along with guidance on setting up the accounts and connecting them to the website.

These graphics will follow the approved website branding to create a consistent appearance across platforms.

Ongoing social media content creation and account management are not included.

Project Timeline & Process

We anticipate approximately two weeks for website design and development once the content and design direction have been approved.

  1. Content & Brand DirectionWe'll review the existing logo concepts, confirm the preferred visual direction, and collect the content required for the website.
  2. Design ReviewWe'll prepare a homepage design for review, incorporating the approved branding and website content. The committee will have an opportunity to provide consolidated feedback before development begins.
  3. Website DevelopmentOnce the design is approved, we'll build the website in WordPress and Elementor, complete mobile optimization, and configure the website's essential features.
  4. Review & LaunchWe'll complete final testing, connect the domain, and launch the website following approval. We'll also provide training so the team can make basic text, image, and link updates independently.

The project includes two rounds of consolidated revisions.

Project scheduling can typically begin within one week of approval, subject to availability and receipt of the required materials.

Planning for Future Growth

The initial website is intended to provide DCD Canada with a starting point while the organization completes its formal establishment.

As the organization grows and additional funding becomes available, the website can evolve to support its broader goals.

Potential future additions include

  • Dedicated resource pages for children, adolescents, and adults
  • Research and educational materials
  • News, events, and awareness campaigns
  • Membership and volunteer opportunities
  • Fundraising and donation integration
  • Expanded content for families, educators, clinicians, and individuals with DCD

These features are not included in the initial launch package but can be added as separate projects when the organization is ready.
