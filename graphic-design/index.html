What I Offer

Whether you’re building a brand from scratch or need fresh, on-brand visuals for your next campaign, I’ve got you covered. Here’s what I can create for you:

Visual Branding

I’ll help you build a cohesive visual identity that makes your brand instantly recognizable. This can include:

Logo Design

Your logo is often the first thing people notice—let’s make it count. My logo design package includes:

Need designs that stop the scroll? I create high-quality graphics that match your brand and get your message across, including:

  • Email headers and presentation slides

The PRocess

Every project is a collaboration. Whether you need a full brand identity or a few polished marketing graphics, we follow a simple, supportive process to bring your vision to life.

Discovery

We’ll start with a 30-minute call to learn more about your business and design needs. It’s a chance to ask questions, align on goals, and make sure we’re a good fit. After the call, you’ll receive a proposal with the scope, timeline, and pricing.

Design

Once approved, we’ll get started on design concepts and send them to you by email. You’ll be able to review, leave feedback, and request revisions. We refine until everything feels just right and aligned with your brand.

DELIVERY

When everything’s finalized, we’ll package up your files and send them your way ready for print, web, or wherever you need to show up. You’ll also get usage tips and light post-project support if needed.
