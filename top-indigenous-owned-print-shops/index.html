Top Indigenous-Owned Print Shops

Leanne Jones September 5, 2025

Key Takeaways

  • Indigenous-owned print shops in Canada provide branding, tradeshow, and promo materials that strengthen business visibility.
  • Supporting Indigenous-owned businesses keeps dollars circulating in our communities and ensures cultural integrity in print and design. Featured Indigenous print shops include:
    • Indigenous Marketing (BC) – Print + marketing services
    • Koda Promo (Saskatchewan) – Promotional products + custom printing
    • Brandigenous (BC) – Branding with cultural identity
    • Dreamcatcher Promotions (Manitoba) – Apparel, gifts, and large-scale promo products

    How Indigenous-Owned Print Shops Support Community

    As an Indigenous graphic designer based in Canada, I know firsthand how powerful print can be for branding, marketing, and community connection. From tradeshow displays to promotional materials and everyday business essentials, print is often the backbone of how we show up professionally.

    That’s why I believe it’s important to support Indigenous-owned print shops whenever possible. Print is such a key part of my own business, and working with other Indigenous companies not only keeps dollars circulating within our communities but also ensures our stories and values are represented authentically.

    Here are a few Indigenous-owned print shops across Canada doing incredible work:

    Indigenous Marketing Solutions

    Based in BC

    Indigenous Marketing is a full-service agency that offers print solutions alongside branding, web design, and marketing services. Their print shop provides everything from business cards and brochures to large-format tradeshow displays, helping Indigenous businesses make a lasting impression.

    Visit Indigenous Marketing Solutions

    Koda Promo

    Based in Saskatchewan

    Koda Promo specializes in custom promotional products and print materials. Whether you’re looking for branded apparel, signage, or event swag, they focus on creating marketing and promo materials that amplify Indigenous voices and help businesses stand out.

    Visit Koda Promo

    Brandigenous

    Based in BC

    Brandigenous combines print and promotional products with a strong focus on cultural identity. They offer creative solutions for businesses that want to express their story through marketing materials, making them a go-to Indigenous print shop for organizations looking to merge branding with culture.

    Visit Brandigenous

    Dreamcatcher Promotions

    Based in Manitoba

    Dreamcatcher Promotions is one of the largest Indigenous-owned promotional product suppliers in Canada. They provide everything from screen-printed apparel to corporate gifts, tradeshow giveaways, and branded merchandise. With a huge selection and strong community presence, they’ve helped countless Indigenous and non-Indigenous organizations with their printing and promo needs.

    Visit Dreamcatcher Promotions
