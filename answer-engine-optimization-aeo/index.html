AI engines don’t just look at keywords. They look for clear, structured, and useful information.

We create and optimize content that is:

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Answer Engine Optimization?
AEO is the process of structuring your website so it can be used as a source in AI-generated answers on platforms like ChatGPT and Google.
How is AEO different from SEO?
SEO focuses on ranking in search results. AEO focuses on being included in the answer itself. The two work together.
Can you guarantee my site will show up in AI answers?
No one can guarantee placement, but we implement proven strategies that significantly increase your chances of being selected as a source.
Do I need to create new content?
In many cases, we can optimize your existing pages. We may also recommend new content to strengthen your coverage on key topics.
Does this replace traditional SEO?
No. AEO builds on SEO. You still need a strong SEO foundation for the best results.
