Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about keeping your WordPress site secure? We’ve got you covered! Here are some of the most common inquiries about WordPress security and SEO and how we help to protect your site. Whether you’re new to website security or need expert advice, we’re here to ensure your site stays safe, updated, and performing at its best.

Why is WordPress Security so important

WordPress is the most widely used website platform, making it a prime target for hackers. Ensuring your site’s security protects your data, prevents downtime, and helps maintain customer trust.

How do I know if my WordPress site is secure?

Regular security scans, updated plugins, and strong password practices are key indicators. We offer thorough security audits to assess your site’s vulnerabilities and provide ongoing monitoring.

What should I do if my site is hacked?
If your WordPress site is hacked, immediate action is required to minimize damage. We provide hack recovery services, restoring your site from recent backups and securing any weaknesses to prevent future attacks. We take daily backups of your site, and also go back 30 days in case you need something from a previous date.
How often should I update my WordPress plugins and themes?
WordPress plugins and themes should be updated regularly to patch vulnerabilities and improve functionality. We manage updates for you, ensuring your site always runs on the latest and safest versions.
What can I do to prevent malware on my WordPress site?

Implementing strong security measures like malware scanning, firewall protection, and routine backups can significantly reduce the risk of malware infections. We monitor your site 24/7 to catch any potential threats early and keep it clean.

If I choose a package with 1 or 2 hours worth of website work, what does that mean?
When you purchase a package with 1 or 2 hours of website design work, you gain the freedom to use it however you like. Whether it’s updating existing copy, redesigning sections, adding new features, optimizing layout, or even refreshing visuals—this time is yours to customize your website to fit your evolving needs.
Can you really monitor my competitors keywords, and help me use them on my own site?
Absolutely! We use advanced software to track how your competitors are ranking and identify the keywords driving their success. By analyzing this valuable data, we can help you optimize your website with the same high-performing keywords, giving you a competitive edge. With this insight, we’ll tailor your content and SEO strategy to improve your rankings, attract more traffic, and position your site to outperform the competition.
