AEO - FAQ SCHEMA

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is a newer layer of SEO focused on helping AI-driven platforms like ChatGPT, Google’s AI results, Perplexity, and other large language models (LLMs) understand and surface your content more easily.

By adding structured FAQ schema to key pages and blog posts, we’re essentially “labeling” important questions and answers in a way that machines can read instantly. This helps search engines and AI tools quickly identify your expertise, increases your chances of being featured in rich results, and improves your visibility when users ask direct questions.

In short, this is code added behind the scenes that makes your content easier to find, easier to trust, and more likely to rank across both traditional search and AI-driven platforms.
