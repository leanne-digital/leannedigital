Executive Summary

September focused on post-launch technical cleanup, structured-data consolidation, tracking accuracy, content corrections, website performance, and preparation for continued answer engine optimization (AEO) improvements.

The work strengthens the foundation that customers, search engines, and AI answer systems rely on: consistent product information, useful answers, clear navigation, reliable measurement, and fewer conflicting signals. These improvements support visibility and a smoother customer experience; this report does not claim a measured increase in rankings, AI citations, leads, or page-speed scores.

The supplied audit flagged 47 pages: 40 long titles, six URL warnings, and one short title. Review and remediation are complete for that list: 41 titles were improved, two mismatched articles were replaced, and four readable URLs were retained after review. All 47 audited URLs loaded successfully with matching canonical addresses after the cleanup. A fresh Ubersuggest crawl is still needed to refresh its dashboard.

Technical Work Completed

Clearer Windows content

Cleaned up the Windows FAQs by removing leftover editorial language and correcting duplicated or mismatched answers. Customers now receive more consistent answers, and the content provides a better foundation for future AEO work.

An accurate four-step process

Replaced the four duplicated “Free Consultation & Evaluation” headings with the correct sequence: consultation; measurements and product selection; installation; final inspection and warranty. The Windows page now explains what customers can expect at each stage.

Cleaner advertising measurement

Removed the duplicate Google Ads AW-760291821 implementation from Elementor Custom Code. This reduces the risk of redundant tracking activity and makes measurement easier to maintain.

Consistent structured data

Removed manually injected LocalBusiness schema that conflicted with Yoast-generated structured data. Located and paused legacy Google Tag Manager schema tags that duplicated sitewide WebSite and SiteNavigationElement markup. Consolidating these signals helps search and answer systems interpret the site consistently.

More selective reviews loading

Updated the custom WP Google Reviews plugin so its CSS and JavaScript load only when a reviews shortcode is rendered. Pages without reviews no longer need those plugin assets, reducing unnecessary browser work.

Spam protection preserved

Verified that Gravity Forms’ sitewide reCAPTCHA v3 loading is intentional and remains enabled for spam-risk scoring. Keeping it available supports lead-form protection.

Webchat confirmed

Verified that Birdeye is the requested sitewide webchat implementation and does not duplicate the Google Reviews system. The customer-contact channel remains available.

Current Marvin product naming

Replaced outdated “Marvin Signature” terminology with “Marvin Ultimate.” Moved the applicable page to /doors/marvin-ultimate-doors/ and removed the old terminology from live content and navigation, helping customers find the correct product range.

Navigation readability

Corrected mobile and dropdown navigation typography so menu choices are easier to read and use across screen sizes.

Homepage display restored

Resolved the temporary homepage header and footer display issue by resetting and clearing the website cache, restoring the expected navigation and page presentation.

Measured backlink response

Reviewed suspicious backlinks with Davis and HeyTony. The agreed approach is to leave obvious spam links alone because there is currently no indication that an urgent disavowal is required. Monitoring continues without an unnecessary intervention.

Additional Technical SEO Cleanup

The audit export is a starting point for review, not an instruction to change every URL or force every title into a fixed character count. Titles were reviewed against page intent, with unnecessary repetition and branding reduced. Google may still choose or shorten its own displayed title.

  • 40 long titles: revised for clarity and focus.
  • One short title: expanded to “Website Sitemap | Davis Window & Door.”
  • Six URL warnings: reviewed individually. Two exposed incorrect article content and metadata; four were wording-based warnings that did not justify moving a live page.
  • No audit-driven URL migrations: all six original addresses were retained. No new 301 redirects, redirect chains, or loops were introduced by this cleanup.

Verification covered all 47 audited addresses: HTTP 200 responses at the original URLs and matching canonical links. All 41 planned title replacements matched the public HTML exactly. The two replacement articles retained their existing URLs, featured images, and page templates, and their added internal links were checked. Because no URL changed, canonical and sitemap destinations did not require migration updates.

Business impact: the search-facing wording is clearer, two articles once again answer the topics promised by their URLs, and existing link equity is preserved. These 47 reviewed items do not establish that the entire website is free of SEO issues. Ubersuggest has not yet been recrawled, so its previous warnings may remain visible until the next audit.
