Project Overview

Oséko:wa is preparing to take an important step into the government procurement space, and this website will be the foundation that supports that growth. The goal is to create a clean, bilingual platform that reflects the company’s professionalism while honoring the story behind it — an Indigenous woman building a trusted and reliable furniture supply business in the Capital Region and across Eastern Ontario.

The website will present Oséko:wa’s services with clarity and confidence, provide space for all required procurement documents, and communicate the credibility needed for government buyers. Every part of the build will be designed with ease of use, transparency, and compliance in mind so Oséko:wa can confidently participate in procurement opportunities and make a strong first impression with agencies and partners.
