Project Overview

Swan Planning Consultancy is a woman-owned planning firm founded by Heather Swan, a seasoned Plangineer (Planner + Engineer) with over 25 years of experience working alongside Indigenous communities, government bodies, and corporate clients across Canada. With a focus on reconciliation, Indigenous inclusion, and relationship-driven collaboration, the brand needed a visual identity that felt grounded, graceful, and meaningful.

Scope of Work

  • Primary Logo
    A thoughtful, elegant mark featuring a stylized swan, subtly inspired by Indigenous design elements and reflective of land and water themes.

  • Secondary Logo
    A horizontal variation ideal for documents, headers, and web applications.

  • Submark
    A compact icon-style logo designed for social media, profile images, and branded collateral.

  • One-Page Brand Style Sheet
    A clean, easy-to-follow brand reference covering logo usage, color palette (earthy and calming tones), and typography selections that convey professionalism with warmth.

Design Approach
The logo suite was built to echo Swan Planning’s core values: Respect, Relationship, Reciprocity, Responsibility, and Reconciliation. The swan—symbolic of transformation and harmony—was paired with gentle curves and nature-inspired lines to represent balance, water, and grounded movement. The resulting identity is approachable yet authoritative, modern yet timeless.

This identity now supports Swan Planning’s mission to advance inclusive, values-driven planning in partnership with Indigenous communities and institutions across Canada.
