Project Overview

This project is focused on creating a clean, professional website for Mr. Pool Guy that reflects the bright, tropical feel of Miami while building trust with customers who need fast, reliable service. The goal is to design a site that not only looks great, but also supports long term visibility with a solid SEO-friendly structure.

The design will take a strategic, customer-first approach. Visitors should immediately understand who you are, what you offer, and how to contact you. The site will use a fresh color palette, simple layouts, and friendly, clear copy that reassures homeowners who might be stressed or dealing with pool issues. Every design choice will help position Mr. Pool Guy as reliable, approachable, and easy to work with.

The website will be organized so customers can quickly find the services they need, whether it’s cleaning, repairs, hot tub support, or regular maintenance. The final result will be a strong, easy-to-use online presence that helps you attract more customers and grow your business.
