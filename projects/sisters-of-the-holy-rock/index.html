Project Overview

Sisters of the Holy Rock

Entertainment and Fundraising Website Design

The Sisters of the Holy Rock are a long running volunteer performance group known for joyful shows, community spirit, and major fundraising impact. They needed a fresh website that felt bright, fun, and welcoming while still being easy to navigate for an older audience.

The Approach

I created a design using bold purples, playful accents, and large, easy to read typography. The goal was to keep the experience lively without overwhelming users. Increasing font sizes, improving spacing, and simplifying layout choices made the site accessible and comfortable to use, especially for visitors who might not be tech savvy.

At the same time, the design respects the group’s history. The tone stays warm, friendly, and community focused, reflecting the spirit of their performances and the heart behind their fundraising work.

Key Features

  • A bright playful look built around purple tones
  • Large, legible typography for accessibility without feeling childish
  • Clear pathways to see upcoming events, book a fundraiser, or join the group
  • A gallery showcasing the joy and energy of past performances
  • A testimonials section highlighting audience experiences
  • Easy to maintain event listings and review submissions

The Result

The final website captures the fun, musical, and heartfelt personality of the Sisters while making it simple for visitors to find events, book shows, or learn more about joining. It supports the group’s mission to bring joy and raise funds for local causes and offers an inviting experience for audiences of all ages.
