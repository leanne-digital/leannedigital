Project Overview

We partnered with Bloc15, a premier event venue in the heart of Oakland, to elevate their online presence with a modern, polished, and conversion focused website. With more than 10,000 square feet of customizable event space, the new site was designed to highlight the venue’s versatility while making it easy for visitors to explore layouts, view amenities, and inquire about bookings.

Our goal was to create a digital experience that reflects the sophistication of the venue while improving usability, clarity, and search visibility. The website showcases Bloc15’s capacity options, sample floor plans, event services, and gallery in a way that guides visitors toward key actions like viewing the venue or booking a tour.

What We Focused On

• Presenting Bloc15 as Oakland’s leading venue for corporate, private, and fundraising events
• Highlighting the venue’s scale, floor plans, and amenities through clean layouts and strong visuals
• Creating a simple user journey with clear navigation and prominent booking calls to action
• Ensuring the site is SEO friendly with proper structure, metadata, and content organization
• Structuring pages so the Bloc15 team can easily update content as new events or offerings evolve

The Outcome

The redesigned Bloc15 website positions the venue as a top choice for event planners and businesses by combining beautiful visuals, intuitive navigation, and a strong SEO foundation. Visitors can quickly imagine their event in the space, explore all offerings, and book a consultation with confidence.
