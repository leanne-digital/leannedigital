My Philosophy

I Don't Believe in Gatekeeping. I Believe in Helping Each Other Succeed.

I've always believed that successful businesses are built around helping people, and sharing knowledge is another way we can do that.

Branding and marketing involve a lot of moving pieces. From fonts and colours to websites, SEO, and messaging, it can feel overwhelming when you're just starting out.

But once you understand the fundamentals, everything starts to connect.

That's what I love about teaching. Making complicated concepts easier to understand so entrepreneurs can put that knowledge to work in their own businesses.

Because when we share what we know, we all have a better chance of succeeding.

  1. 01

    Branding & Marketing

    Why Branding & Marketing Work Better Together

    Branding and marketing are often treated as two separate things, but I believe they're most powerful when they work together.

    Branding is more than a pretty logo. It's how we communicate, build trust, and connect with our customers. Marketing helps people discover what we have to offer.

    Takeaway

    Understanding how branding and marketing work together can help you build a stronger, more recognizable business.

  2. 02

    Digital Marketing & SEO

    Digital Marketing Made Simple for Small Businesses

    Marketing can feel overwhelming, especially when you're just getting started. Between websites, SEO, and social media, where do you even begin?

    I break down digital marketing into practical concepts that make sense, helping entrepreneurs understand how to reach their audience and grow their online presence.

    Takeaway

    If you're worried about being annoying with your marketing, you probably need to be twice as annoying as you think you are!

  3. 03

    Entrepreneurship

    Entrepreneurship, Confidence & Personal Growth

    I've always said entrepreneurship is personal growth on steroids.

    When I started Leanne Digital, I quickly learned that running a design business involves a surprising amount of time doing everything except designing!

    As a naturally quiet person and mom of two boys, building my business has pushed me outside my comfort zone and taught me that confidence is a skill we develop through practice.

    Takeaway

    You don't need to have everything figured out to start building something meaningful.

  4. 04

    Indigenous Entrepreneurship

    Indigenous Entrepreneurship, Community & Storytelling

    As a member of Peguis First Nation, I'm passionate about supporting and uplifting Indigenous entrepreneurs.

    Relationships, community, and storytelling are central to how I approach business. I believe these values are strengths we can build successful businesses around, not things we need to set aside.

    I'm excited about the future of Indigenous entrepreneurship and the incredible businesses we'll continue to see grow.

    Takeaway

    You can build a successful business while staying true to your values, your community, and your story.

About Leanne

Agency. In-House. Business Owner. I've Lived All Three Lives.

I've spent over a decade working in branding, design, and digital marketing, experiencing the creative industry from just about every angle.

Agency life taught me how to work with different businesses and industries. Working in-house showed me how design and marketing support long-term business goals.

And becoming a business owner? Well, that's taught me just about everything else!

In 2023, I founded Leanne Digital, growing it from freelance work into a creative studio serving businesses and organizations across Canada.

I'm also a proud member of Peguis First Nation, a Pow Wow Pitch semi-finalist, an industry mentor, and a mom of two boys.

Entrepreneurship has opened doors I never imagined, and I'm excited to keep learning, growing, and sharing that knowledge with others.
